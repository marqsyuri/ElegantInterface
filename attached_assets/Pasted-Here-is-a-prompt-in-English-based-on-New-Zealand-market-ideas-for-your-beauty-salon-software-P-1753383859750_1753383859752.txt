**Prompt:**

Imagine you are designing a next-generation software solution for managing a beauty salon in New Zealand. The salon offers services such as haircuts, hair coloring and tinting, hair treatments, hair extensions, blowouts, and nail care. Your goal is to create a system that not only simplifies daily management but also enhances the customer experience and helps the salon grow in 2025.

What additional features and innovations would you include in this software to differentiate it in the New Zealand market? Consider functionalities such as:  
- Smart online appointment scheduling with automatic reminders  
- Detailed client profiles with service history and personalized recommendations  
- Integrated electronic payments and point-of-sale (POS) systems  
- Automated marketing tools including email/SMS campaigns and loyalty programs  
- Real-time inventory and stock management with low-stock alerts  
- Staff management including scheduling, commission tracking, and performance reports  
- Comprehensive dashboards with analytics on sales, popular services, and customer trends  
- Mobile app access for clients to book, manage appointments, and receive promotions  
- Sustainability tools for monitoring product usage and reducing waste  
- Integration with social media platforms and Google booking features  

Describe these features, how they would benefit both salon management and clients, and highlight key trends in beauty salon software for 2025 specific to New Zealand’s market.

This prompt can help inspire or guide detailed feature development aligned with modern trends and client expectations in New Zealand’s beauty industry.
